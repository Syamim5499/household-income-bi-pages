The marker and bars share a scale that expands for the amount entered. Local median differences measure RM and percentage above/below the median; they do not by themselves determine a percentile.

How to read the metrics

MeasureMeaning
B40 / M40 / T20National household income groups using rounded 2024 cutoffs: below RM5,860; RM5,860–12,679; RM12,680 and above.
MedianHalf of surveyed households have income on either side of this measure.

From DOSM data to this chart

1 · Extract

Python downloads five official OpenDOSM CSV datasets: national income and percentiles, state income and percentiles, and district income.

2 · Transform

It validates columns, selects the latest common survey year, filters Selangor and Kuala Langat, builds percentile bands, and derives rounded national cutoffs.

3 · Publish

It writes a compact data/income.json. This static page reads the JSON and performs comparisons in your browser after you press Go.

Run python3 scripts/build_data.py to regenerate the JSON. The repository includes the full source and a scheduled GitHub Action. A new survey year is selected only when all sources include it.

Sources and limits

Malaysia · State · District · State percentiles · 2024 survey report

Gross income includes household income beyond wages. Survey data are nominal and represent their stated year, not live 2026 income distribution. District percentile bands are unavailable here; the percentile display is for Selangor. The public page stores no entered amount remotely.
